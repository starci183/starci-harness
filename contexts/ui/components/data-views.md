# Data views

Contract: [data-views](../../grammars/contract.md#data-views).

## Meter reading voice
`Meter labelTone="muted"` is the quiet reading of a secondary gauge (a usage line at the foot of a rail, under a footer): the label and the value muted, 12 regular; the default stays the foreground label at 14 medium. It colours words only (`tone` colours the fill). Not made the default: a headline gauge keeps its
label. Evidence (Rule 1): NIVO console shell canvas, repository `nivoos-lite` at `8759c8ab`, `.starcistacks/canvas/pages/01-shell-app.html``:208` ("Credit tháng này 7.200 / 10.000": a muted 12 label and value over a 4px bar). Story `Navigation/Shell gaps (0.46.0)` `QuietMeter`.

## Bars follow HeroUI ProgressBar sizes
Every bar-like data mark (`Meter` and its segments, `Progress` and its steps, `ProportionBar`, a `FunnelChart` stage, the ext
`DonutChart` ring) is HeroUI v3 ProgressBar's own track: sm 4px, md 8px (the default), lg 12px, at the vendor's corner for that
size (a quarter, a half, three quarters of `--radius`), never a full pill and never a height of the grammar's own; a donut's ring is
the md track thick, its diameter a chart size (64, 120, 160). The steps are the `--grammar-bar-*` tokens (owner: `@starci/grammar`
`src/common/ramps.css` "BARS"; story shots at 390 and 1440). Why: a bar is read against the vendor's own progress bars on the
same screen, and a grammar-invented height reads as a second component. The empty track keeps the measures' one neutral
(`--starci-core-track`, [toast-alert](toast-alert.md)), since the vendor's `--default` vanishes on a white theme.

## Job
Draw a set of items as stages, as entries on two axes, or as a few amounts to compare, in the four states of a region.

## Choose

Choice `data-views.choose.view`: which view a set of items takes. The first row that applies wins.

| Row | When | Choose | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| board | the items move through named stages and the reader scans each stage | `Board`: one column per stage with its count, each item an object card; an empty stage says so in place; a card is dragged (grip on hover and focus, origin slot, lifted card, drop slot with the stage's `dropHint`, an `endBand` after the last stage) and a stage answers a drop `boardAllow`, `boardRefuse(reason)` or `boardNeedsData(kind)` (list-table.md "Board") | `DNA-COMMON-BOARD-ONE-WIDTH` |
| schedule | each item belongs to a time or a place on two axes (a week by hour, a month, people by day) | `ScheduleGrid`: columns and rows named, items placed by both; a phone reads it as an agenda; an entry from a list beside the grid is dragged in with `startScheduleDrag` from the `DragItem` and arrives at `onDrop` with source `outside`, and the keyboard path is to pick it in the list and press a cell (`onCellPress`) | `DNA-COMMON-SCHEDULE-HEADINGS` |
| bars | a handful of amounts is compared on one scale (per day, per channel) | `BarChart` of the charts extension ([charts](charts.md)); core draws no chart | [charts](charts.md) |
| trend | the shape of one measure over time, small enough to sit beside a number or in a row | `Sparkline` of the charts extension ([charts](charts.md)); under a figure it is `Figure.trendChart` (the app passes the ext `Sparkline`) | [charts](charts.md) |
| segments | several wholes are told against each other, each split the same way (leads by channel and how many booked, hours by person and kind) | `SegmentBar`: a row per whole on ONE scale (the longest row is the full track), the track split into the series' segments, the row's own reading at its end, one legend of the series under the rows; `quiet` makes the first series the ink and the rest the muted step | candidate |
| proportion | the parts of one whole are told at a glance (receivables by age, a share by channel) | `ProportionBar`: one track split by share, a legend with the dot, the label and the amount as real text; no axis, no percentage; the track is HeroUI ProgressBar's (`size` md 8px by default, sm 4px, lg 12px, the vendor's corner, never a pill) | candidate |
| ranges | a few rows each carry a measured range and its best reading, read against one reference row (variants against the control, plans against the current one) | `IntervalChart`: a row per label with its range as a bar in the row's soft tone, the point as a mark in the tone itself, one dashed line through every row at the reference row's point (`isReference`; its label says so in words), the value as real text at the row's end and the two scale ends under the plots; `tone` per row is the app's reading, never the chart's verdict; below 33rem of chart width each row stacks, the label and value over the plot | `DNA-COMMON-INTERVAL-CHART-ONE-SCALE`, `DNA-COMMON-INTERVAL-CHART-REFERENCE-ONE-LINE`, `DNA-COMMON-INTERVAL-CHART-STACKS-NARROW` |
| funnel | the items go through ordered stages and the reader asks how many reach each and how many fall away between two (leads by stage, orders by step) | `FunnelChart`: per stage a text row ("label · count", the step into it, the down arrow icon and "60%", the share of the stage before that came through, and `value` at its end) over a HeroUI ProgressBar md track (8px), every track the chart's width and every fill from one edge, measured against the largest stage, in the stage's solid `tone` (the app's reading); the step that lost the largest share is the danger ink, and `stepLabel` writes a step's words; no stages, or every stage at zero, is `empty`; stages are given in order, never sorted by the chart. A count that leaves the funnel sideways (lost, cancelled) is not a stage: it is the `lost` bucket (label, count, the app's split in `note`), drawn after the last stage and never part of the scale; `layout="steps"` is the compact funnel of ONE row (a column per stage: name, count with the share that came through, a short track), side by side from 36rem of chart width and the rows layout below it; `quiet` takes the fills to the ink | `DNA-COMMON-FUNNEL-CHART-ONE-SCALE`, `DNA-COMMON-FUNNEL-CHART-ONE-START`, `DNA-COMMON-FUNNEL-CHART-STACKS-NARROW` |
| timeline | each item is a stretch of time on one axis (a plan by day, a roadmap by week) with a start and an end, and the reader asks what overlaps, how far along it is and what waits on what | `ScheduleGrid` with `endColumn` on the entry: one bar over the columns it crosses in its row (overlapping bars of one row take their own lines), `progress` 0 to 1 fills it from its start (say the share in `meta`), `form="milestone"` draws a diamond in one column, `after` lists the entries it waits on and an arrow runs from the end of each, a column's `todayAt` draws the line for now in place of the tint, a row with `isGroup` folds the rows whose `parent` is it (`isCollapsed`, `onRowToggle`; the app keeps the state), and `resize` puts handles on both ends that move an end by drag or by the arrow keys of the focused handle (`onResize`); under 48rem the table is the agenda and each bar names the columns it covers | `DNA-COMMON-SCHEDULE-SPAN-STARTS-IN-ITS-CELL`, `DNA-COMMON-SCHEDULE-SPAN-ENDS-IN-ITS-CELL`, story `Navigation & Data/Schedule timeline` |
| key | a view colours its entries by a status and the reader needs the words of each colour | `Legend`: a mark in the tone then its label, on one line that wraps when narrow; it explains tones and takes no press (a filter is `Tabs kind="filter"`); `form="milestone"` draws the diamond mark | `DNA-COMMON-LEGEND-ONE-MARK-SIZE`, story `Navigation & Data/Legend` |
| map | each item is a place with a count (stores by area, visits by district) and the reader asks where they gather | `GeoMap`: points with a count on a raster tile ground the app serves (`tiles.url` with `{z}/{x}/{y}`, `attribution` required; no key, no vendor call of the grammar's own; a PMTiles archive is served by the app as tile images), pan and zoom by drag, buttons, keys, pinch or Ctrl/Command with the wheel, crowded points merge into a cluster that zooms in on press, a single point is a button when `onSelect` is given, and a ranked list of every point is the reading for assistive technology; a node graph is not a Grammar component (app-owned, inside a `Custom` region) | story `Navigation & Data/Geo map` (its play functions prove the clusters, the keys and the list) |
| list | the items have no stage, place or comparison | `Section kind="list"` ([list-table](list-table.md)) | judgement |
| figures | one to five numbers each stand alone | `Section kind="figures"`, the ribbon ([composition](../composition.md) COLLECTION-4); a figure may carry `trend` (`up`, `down`, `flat`, with `trendSense`, `trendLabel`) and `trendChart` (the ext `Sparkline`, passed drawn: a neutral grey line, none for no data or a flat-zero series; the meta words carry the direction in the status ink); the value is the display size 20, its label and meta 14 | judgement |

**Status inside a view (the status-mark rule; [identity-mark](identity-mark.md) "Status marks").** A status word inside a view (a legend entry, a graph edge label, a cell, a board card's state) is the inline status (A), `InlineStatus`: the bare glyph (24px beside 14px words, 20px in a 12px line) and its words in the state's ink, no plate. A node or a row that leads with a mark takes the round 40px tile (B), the subject icon on the state's plate (a graph node's kind, a workflow), and its status trails as (A); a run's steps read as a `Timeline` with the status glyph. Never two large circles side by side.

Choice `data-views.choose.state`: how a view answers while it is not ready. The first row that applies wins.

| Row | When | Choose | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| empty | the read finished with nothing | the `empty` slot, one action at most ([empty-state](empty-state.md)) | `DNA-COMMON-EMPTY-ACTIONS` |
