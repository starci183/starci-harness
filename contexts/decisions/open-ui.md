# Open UI decisions

Choices made while writing `knowledge/ui`, each awaiting the owner's feedback. Each records the question, the
choice the rules now state, the alternative, the reason, and what must change elsewhere for no app to be able to
decide it two ways. When the owner confirms one, its ruling id goes to [rulings.yaml](rulings.yaml) and the entry
leaves this file.

| Id | Question | Chosen (now the rule) | Alternative | Reason | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UI-1 | Order of a decision group | way out, alternative, commit; commit last at every width (CTA-5) | commit first (legacy CTA-5 case-1, the Nivo example) | matches the vendor dialog footer, the contract's intent map, the lint and CTA-6's confirm dialog; stacked, the commit lands last, nearest the thumb (UX-9) | none |
| UI-2 | How many primaries a view holds | one `commit` per view (page, or the open modal); every other next step is `neutral` (CTA-1) | one primary per decision region, several per view (legacy ACCENT-1 case-4) | legacy TASTE-5 counted one accent fill per view while ACCENT-1 allowed one per region: two readings. One commit per view matches the focal-point rule and `single-commit-per-render` | none |
| UI-3 | Type-size cap | per region at most 3 sizes and 2 weights; per view at most 4 sizes (`type.region`, `type.view`) | one cap of 3 (legacy TASTE-6) or "3 or 4" (legacy DISCIPLINE-2) | a page needs page heading, section heading, body and meta (four ranks), while one card needs three; the two legacy numbers were counting different scopes | the proof brief reads both keys |
| UI-4 | Destructive paint | `danger-soft` at entry, solid `danger` only at the confirm inside an `AlertDialog` (CTA-4) | words plus a danger glyph, never paint (the Nivo example P1) | the contract, the lint and BRAND-1 all carry a danger fill; the example was a product preference | the product's `.starciwork` drops P1's "never paint" |
| UI-5 | The control ramp | three steps `sm` < `md` < `lg` in equal increments, `md` the one default, `control` = the `md` height held for bar controls ([../ui/scale.md](../ui/scale.md)); reference values sm 32, md 40, lg 48 px; no desktop step-down; fields `md` only | keep the vendor's per-family sizes (36 px fields, 4 px desktop step-down) | a row is read as one object; mixed heights read as misalignment; the vendor's only ladder on 8 px steps (Avatar) is extended to every control | contract (K1): one size token set for sm, md, lg assigned in the shared sheet (the legacy `--starci-core-control-min-size` was never assigned and had about 60 fallbacks); SegmentedControl publishes `sm`, `md` (legacy `control` 2.75 rem and vendor `md`/`lg` dropped); TagGroup default `sm`; Input stays `md`; decide whether `control` stays a prop value or is set only by bar context |
| UI-6 | 40 vs 44 px | drawn height 40 (`md`) and target size are separate facts; target floor 44 x 44 on a coarse pointer or narrow viewport, 24 x 24 on a fine pointer, met by the hit area, for every interactive target (`target.coarse`, `target.fine`) | 44 drawn everywhere; or a 40 floor everywhere; or 44 only for accordion triggers and rail controls (legacy A11Y-4) | 44 drawn breaks vendor parity and desktop density; a 40 floor is below touch guidance; the legacy floor applied to two components while TASTE-11 and UX-9 cited it for all | contract (K1): a target-floor token and a hit-area extension on every pressable part; rows of `sm` controls take `md` on coarse pointers |
| UI-7 | May a KPI band be a painted (ink or accent) slab | never: figures stay on a default card's stat strip; a full-strength slab carries no copy (ACCENT-6, DISCIPLINE-2, SURFACE-6) | an ink KPI band as the overview's signature moment (the Nivo example) | copy on a slab breaks the surface/foreground pairing, competes with the commit ("two reds") and spends the accent budget on figures | the product's `.starciwork` moves its KPIs off the ink band |
| UI-8 | Group glyph beside a conversation title | none: the composite avatar carries the group meaning (COLLECTION-3, FE-APP-SHELL-7) | a group marker beside the name (legacy FE-APP-SHELL-7) | two carriers for one fact | none |
| UI-9 | Tabs looks and their names | the look follows placement (page tabs outside a painted surface, region tabs inside), resolved by the grammar from nesting; apps never pass the variant | apps pass `variant`; keep the grammar names inverted against the vendor's (legacy grammar `primary` = vendor `secondary`) | removes a choice the app could make two ways and a vocabulary trap | contract (K1): name the looks by placement or keep the vendor names, never inverted; lint refuses an app-set tabs variant like `nested-control-variant` does for fields |
| UI-10 | Field variant | resolved by the grammar from the layer; apps omit it | a static `secondary` inside cards (legacy COLOR-3 case-6, the example) | one owner per fact; already enforced by `nested-control-variant` | none |
| UI-11 | Accent on identity tiles and app-owned marks | neutral; status tone only for a real outcome (ACCENT-2) | legacy radius examples rendered `bg-accent-soft` tiles and markers | the examples contradicted ACCENT-2 and ACCENT-6 | none: the examples are gone |
| UI-12 | Radius vocabulary | the app names a corner role (tiny, compact, control, surface, hero, pill); the grammar sets values from one base radius | literal utility classes with rem values (legacy radius file) | a literal freezes one grammar's value into every grammar | contract (K1): publish the corner roles as tokens |
| UI-13 | Where thresholds live | one key per number in `ui/proof/thresholds.yaml`, shared by the composition rule and the proof criterion | numbers inside prose, parsed by regex (legacy `ui-proof-brief`) | rewording a sentence silently disabled a gate; the empty padding scale silently degraded TASTE-4 | the proof brief reads thresholds by key and the spacing steps from the contract |
| UI-14 | Grammar names | grammars `core` (the legacy core, kept faithfully) and `offset-pop`; `heritage` and a separate `starci` family dropped; the legacy catalog id `starci` means `core` | keep `starci`, `heritage` | owner ruling relayed by the coordinator | removed-vocabulary entry for `starci` as a family id (K1/K3) |
| UI-15 | The worked brand-direction example | dropped from knowledge; it belongs to the Nivo product's `.starciwork` | a product-free skeleton example here | it is a product record (mascot, palette, locale, page archetypes) | move the legacy `knowledge/ui/examples/brand-direction.example.yaml` to that product |
| UI-16 | Rule ids after merges | surviving ids keep their address; folded ids are closed and never reused: `ACTION-2`, `STATE-2` into CTA-3; `ACTION-3` into CTA-2; `ACCENT-1` into CTA-1; the `PADDING`/`MARGIN` steps keep their ids in one table; `componentOwnership` tables dropped | renumber freely | grammar `data-contract` stamps and the generated rule catalog cite ids | the rule-catalog generator reads the Markdown headings (`### ID title`) and table ids instead of YAML |
| UI-17 | Lint that names grammar components in the universal canon | knowledge cites `starci-fe/*` and `starci-grammar/*` rule ids as they are today | — | the package split (grammar-common lint) is K1/K3's | update the cited ids when the lint packages are renamed |
