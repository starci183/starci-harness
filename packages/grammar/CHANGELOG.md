## 0.64.7 - 2026-11-28

- Changed (owner 2026-11-28 "quote bự lên chứ, có icon quote, và border left no round"): `Quote variant="block"` is a pull quote: Tabler `quote` mark (new `TablerQuote`) in the accent in its own column, the words at `lg` (one step above the body), a straight 3px accent rule on the start edge and square corners. The rule is the accent at 3px and no app hook reaches it: `--starci-core-quote-edge` no longer touches the block, so an app that thinned it keeps the block's rule. `variant="line"` is unchanged.
- Changed (owner 2026-11-28 "hover phải là tooltip của heroui chứ?"): `GlossaryTerm` shows its definition in HeroUI's Tooltip, the same overlay and look as Grammar's `Tooltip`: above the term with an arrow, after a 250 ms rest of the pointer with HeroUI's fade, on keyboard focus and on a tap (a phone has no hover; a second tap, Escape or leaving closes it). It is a portal, so it no longer covers the line below the term. The trigger is an inline span with the button role (no `<button>` inside a paragraph); `.starci-core-glossary-term` and `.starci-core-glossary-term-definition` are gone, `.starci-core-glossary-term-word` stays.
- Added (h-grammar-code-shiki): client highlighting for `CodeBlock`. `useCodeTokens(code, language)` of `@starci/grammar/core/ext/markdown/react` returns Shiki's tokens made in the browser for the block's `tokens`: Shiki loads by a dynamic import on first use (a page that never draws code never downloads it), with the same highlighter and css-variables theme as the server's `highlightCode`, so the colours are the code palette's in light and dark. `undefined` until ready, for no language, for one Shiki does not ship and if Shiki fails to load: the block draws plain text meanwhile; `highlightLines`, line numbers and Copy are unchanged on top of the tokens. Results are kept per page (64 texts) so a reopened block colours at once. The core stays free of Shiki (the hook lives in the markdown ext).
- Added: `CodeBlock diff` (in place of `code`; `CodeBlockSource`): one change as rows (`CodeDiffLine`: `{ kind: "add" | "del" | "same", text }`) or a unified diff string (`git diff` output; file and hunk headers skipped). Added rows on the code palette's inserted tint, removed rows on its `deleted` tint, a `+`/`−` gutter in the side's ink that is never selected or copied, each changed row's kind in visually hidden words for a screen reader (`labels.added`, `labels.removed`), the header counting both (`labels.changes`, `2 added · 1 removed`, on the folded row too). `tokens` colour the rows (highlight `diffCode(diff)`); Copy copies the code after the change. New helpers `parseUnifiedDiff`, `diffRows`, `diffCode`, `diffAfter`; `tokenSpans` of `MarkdownArticle` is exported for the diff rows. Stories `Content/Code palette (0.55.1)/CodeBlockDiff` and `CodeBlockClientShiki` (real Shiki in the browser: TS with marked lines, bash, an unknown language, a diff; light and dark).
- Added (owner 2026-11-27 "icon tile cũng hỗ trợ số trong mình chứ? để làm step by step"): the step tile (D), `IconTile step={n}` (1–99, clamped; exclusive with `source`, `state` and `activity`): the number on the identity tile's plate and tone, in the Grammar's font at the medium weight with tabular figures, as tall as the glyph (20 on `row`, 24 on `head`); one `role="img"` named `ariaLabel`, default `Step N`. For a step-by-step list: the current step `tone="accent"`, a pending one `neutral`, a finished one the status mark (`state="done"`). Story `Status marks (0.55.0)/StepTiles` (a 3-step list in its three moments and both sizes, light and dark).

## 0.64.0 - 2026-11-26

- Changed (h-grammar-flowgraph-midpoint, owner 2026-11-26): `FlowGraph` lays a flow out on its spine by default (`layout: "spine"`, `layoutSpine` in `spine.ts`, its numbers `SPINE`). The longest path along plain and "yes" edges is one straight centred column; side roads stand in columns to its right at `spine + lane * (NODE_WIDTH + 80)` (`COLUMN_PITCH`); a parallel fork splits its branches evenly (`lane = i - (n - 1) / 2`); one layer is one row. Edges attach only at the middles of the sides (in at the top-mid or, for a loop, the left-mid; out at the bottom-mid, the right-mid for a side road, the left-mid for a loop) with the fewest bends: straight down a column, one bend into a side column or back into the spine, a U on the left for a loop, fork and join bars. Two edges into one node meet at a merge dot above it; three or more drop straight down their own columns into a merge bar over the target that carries the one neutral arrow; loops into one node gather in one left lane. Horizontal runs lie in the gaps between rows, `edgeSpacing` apart. Words stand beside the first segment of their edge, never on a bend. The spine always runs top to bottom; `layout: "layered"` keeps ELK's layout (`LR` or `TB`, ports on the cards).
- Added: edge kinds (`GraphEdge.kind`, `GraphEdgeKind`, `GRAPH_EDGE_KINDS`): `ok` solid neutral, `yes` solid success and stronger, `no` grey dots, `loop` accent dashes with the loop mark (`LOOP_MARK`) before its words, `timeout` round amber dots, `refused` and `declined` thin red dashes, `parallel` neutral from a fork bar. Classes `starci-graph-edge--kinded` and `starci-graph-edge--kind-<kind>` (`edgeKindClassName`); a legend item takes a kind (`{ label, edge: "timeout" }`). The variants only gained: `GraphEdgeVariant` is unchanged.
- Added: shapes read from a step's kind on the spine (`shapeOf`, `FlowNodeData.shape`; `FlowShape` gains `hexagon`, `pill`, `stadium`, `rect`, `call`; `FlowKind` gains `switch`, `parallel`, `call`): an action a rounded card, an if a diamond, a switch a flat hexagon with its `count` badge and one exit into a hollow split bar (`Bar.look`), each case (`case`, `default` kinds; `FlowPort.isDefault`) dropping down its own column, the default rightmost and dotted, the taken case bold after a check (`CHECK_MARK`); a wait a pill with a clock when it has no icon; an end a stadium; a parallel step a square rectangle with solid bars; a call a dashed hexagon. Ports stay at the middles of each box's sides. Stories `Switch` and `ShapesLegend`.

- Added: `collapse` on `FlowGraph` (`FlowCollapse`, `foldFlow`, `FOLD_PREFIX`, `DEFAULT_FOLD_ABOVE`): past 20 steps the side roads the run did not take fold behind one "+N" pill each (the app's words), the spine and the reached steps stay drawn, and pressing a pill unfolds its road. Stories `LongRun35` and `Replies57`.
- Added: `onNodeOpen(id)` on `FlowGraph` and `FlowStepDrawer`, the step's drawer: the core `Drawer` on the right, `wide`, with the step's name, its `version` badge and `status`, then the app's parts in one fixed order (`FLOW_STEP_PARTS`: `in`, `code`, `mutation`, `out`, `state`), each a titled `Section`.

A press on a control inside a card (its title button, a link, its menu) never starts a drag. Offset Pop's press travel drops its transition under reduced motion.
